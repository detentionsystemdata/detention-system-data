# Sources & licensing

Every value in this dataset comes from a public source and is published with its source
and date. Below is our good-faith understanding of each source's terms — not legal advice.
If you believe a source's terms are represented incorrectly here, please let us know (see
the README).

## Sources used

**University of Washington Center for Human Rights — `ice-detain`**
<https://github.com/UWCHR/ice-detain>
FOIA-derived records of ICE detention facilities and field-office areas of responsibility.
The repository's code is licensed GPL-3.0; the underlying data are factual records of a
government agency. Published here with attribution to UWCHR and to the FOIA/ICE origin.

**U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE)**
<https://www.ice.gov/detention-facilities> · <https://www.ice.gov/detain/detention-management> · <https://www.ice.gov/contact/field-offices>
The detention-facility roster, the facility-level detention-statistics workbook, and ERO field-office contact details. Works
of the U.S. federal government are not subject to copyright (17 U.S.C. § 105) and are in the
public domain. The ICE seal and any third-party material are not reused.

**Executive Office for Immigration Review (EOIR), U.S. Department of Justice**
<https://www.justice.gov/eoir/immigration-court-operational-status>
The immigration-court list — names, addresses, and operational status. A U.S. federal
government work, public domain. DOJ seals are not reused.

## How licensing is applied

- Public-domain and openly-licensed values are republished with their citation and date.
- A value from an all-rights-reserved source is **not republished**; in its place the record
  carries an attributed link to the source. This is enforced when each release is built.
- Derived values — facility-to-court co-location hints, cross-source matches, and the
  suggested primary among conflicting values — are **labeled as derivations**, never
  presented as a source's own assertion.
